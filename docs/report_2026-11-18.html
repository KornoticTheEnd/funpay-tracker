How are sales estimated?

FunPay does not publish sales, so they are estimated from stock going down on the same listing between two hourly snapshots. Many sellers list fake amounts or keep editing their stock, so these drops are ignored:

  • Placeholder listings - any listing that ever shows 50,000 gold or more (e.g. 1,000,000,000). These are also left out of "listed stock".
  • Juggled listings - refilled 6+ times, with refills adding up to at least 60% of the drops (stock bouncing between the same few values).
  • Quick refills - a drop that is topped back up to the old amount within 6 hours is treated as an edit. Because of this, the last few hours can still be revised down.
  • Implausible single drops - more than 5,000 gold or more than €2,000 in one hour.

Real sales that are restocked quickly are missed too, so the figures lean low. Treat them as a trend indicator, not exact revenue.
