How are sales estimated?

FunPay does not publish sales, so they are estimated from stock going down on the same offer between two snapshots (taken every hour). A seller lowering stock by hand counts as a sale, restocks between snapshots hide sales, and offers removed entirely are not counted. Stock listed at 1,000,000 or more is treated as a placeholder and ignored. Treat the figures as a trend indicator rather than exact revenue.
